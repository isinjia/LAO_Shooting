   pre-lore "familiar wide-formation fan". blastRadius was a hidden
   number; draw was still a long fire jet.

   `sightbeam || tracer || lockround` as the same LANCE hit burst.
   The slug flew at rifle speed with no drop — felt like the old gold dart.

4) Select-screen Playwright in tick1 only read dossier labels
   (KIT_VERIFY_OK). In-stage firing was explicitly not exercised.

Grep now: Hirume/Constantia spawn paths do not emit `sunbird`, `sightbeam`,
or SHOT/HOLD `homing`. `sunbird`/`sightbeam` remain in shared VFX tables
as unused legacy kits so other draw branches do not explode. Bori C still
