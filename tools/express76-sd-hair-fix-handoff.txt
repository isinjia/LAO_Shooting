ROOT CAUSE
================================================================
Express76 in-game SD looked bald (goggles on a skin-tone scalp) because the
baker never painted the GPU hair meshes.

Unity prefab (Model_PECS_Express76_N):
  EX_parts_head     SpriteRenderer  active  — face/scalp only (no hair fill)
  EX_parts_hair_1   SkinnedMeshRenderer  sortingOrder +5   (front hair / bangs)
  EX_parts_hair_2   SkinnedMeshRenderer  sortingOrder -15  (back ponytail)
  EX_parts_h        SpriteRenderer  m_IsActive 0  — grab/skill overlay, not idle

Hair meshes live in 3d_mesh/Model_PECS_Express76_N_mesh.FBX
  (fileIDs 4300008 / 4300006, guid a8ba0b2e95155d1469ee48e4295aad67).

Their material is 3d_mesh/Materials/Model_PECS_Express76_Part1.mat
  (guid 60a0884212bc55949b319f5fd391ed5a) → Part1.png atlas
  (guid 88dfb0cfb75770f4d906d468901ed6c5).

bake_sd_character.py only scanned model/materials/*.mat. Express76's
lowercase materials/ folder has PNG atlases only (no .mat). SkinnedMesh
pose() then hit `if not texpath: continue` and silently dropped hair
(and also EX_parts_chest / EX_parts_necklace / EX_parts_line).

Griffin/Sleipnir keep .mat next to sprite atlases in Materials/, plus extra
SpriteRenderer hair pieces (hair_F_0, Hair_Top, …), so they baked complete.

Select portrait assets/ui/char-select/express76.png is FormationIcon
(FormationIcon_PECS_Express76_N) and already has dark hair — not rebaked.

Did not invent hair. Did not force-enable inactive EX_parts_h.
