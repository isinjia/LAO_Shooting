ROOT CAUSE (die momentary shrink)
================================================================
bake_sd_character.py framed die with PER-FRAME body AABB (same branch as skill),
while idle/move used a shared locomotion AABB. Collapsing die poses reframed the
body (zoom in/out), so ClipAnimator.draw at fixed drawSize + contentBox made the
death flash look momentarily smaller/larger than idle.

Secondary: clip-anim.js fallback unionContentBox sampled die frames; flatten/FX
expanded the box (Labiata-style) and shrank locomotion. Griffin/Sleipnir had no
baked contentBox, so they hit this path.

NOT caused by: deathFlash alpha, hitScale, or player.hitR (still fixed 4).
